Left hand: settings

Left hand: hold a finger count to open that setting, tilt the hand up / down to move through it, then close the fist to select. Volume and tempo are heard as you move; pattern, sound and variation only change on the fist. In Pattern, twisting the wrist left / right sets how many octaves an arpeggio spans (1 / 3; centred = 2); in Variation it picks sus2 / sus4. Holding a different count instead switches to that setting without saving. Variation and sus go back to a plain triad on every new chord. You can also click any option.
